Why do experienced investors continue developing multifamily housing in challenging markets? Because well-executed real estate can create multiple return engines at once - from cash flow and rent growth to loan paydown, appreciation, tax advantages, and the benefits of prudent leverage. Over time, these forces can compound into meaningful wealth - provided investors buy wisely, manage risk, and have the resilience to hold through difficult markets.
