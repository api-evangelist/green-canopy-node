Seattle's housing conversation has long focused on how many homes the city needs—but an equally important question is who gets the opportunity to own them. A new 10-home affordable ownership project near the Mt. Baker light rail station brings together Africatown Community Land Trust, HomeSight, b9 Architects and Green Canopy NODE to create permanently affordable homeownership opportunities.
