SEATTLE, Washington (August 13, 2024) - Green Canopy NODE, a leading full-service development partner specializing in sustainable housing, is pleased to announce the appointment of Emily Dawson to its Board of Directors. With this addition, the board now consists of six members. Emily Dawson, a licensed architect and mass timber expert, leads the way in bio-based structural solutions, aiming to transform buildings to behave more like plants and industries like forests.
