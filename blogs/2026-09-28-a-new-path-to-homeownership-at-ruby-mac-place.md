Ruby-Mac Place in Portland, Oregon, is showing how affordable homeownership, sustainable design and community wealth-building can work together. Led by Self Enhancement, Inc. (SEI), the 49-home community will provide affordable homes for purchase, giving families an opportunity to build equity and generational wealth.
